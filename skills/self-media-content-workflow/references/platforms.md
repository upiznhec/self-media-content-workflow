# Platform selection

## Smallest platform mix

Recommend one main platform and at most two extra platforms for the core topic:

| What the topic is like | Main platform | Optional extras | Main job |
|---|---|---|---|
| A new claim, a short lesson or a hands-on test of a trend | X | Xiaohongshu | Test the idea quickly, then package it for search |
| Process, comparison, failure or strong visual proof | Xiaohongshu | X, short video | Be found by new users and show the real process |
| An in-depth method, an engineering breakdown or a long-term view | WeChat Official Account | X | Build trust and long-term search value |
| Personality, a demonstration, one clear claim | WeChat Channels or Douyin | Xiaohongshu | Use voice and picture to build trust |
| Videos people search for over a long time, such as science, history or brand stories | YouTube | Douyin, WeChat Channels or Xiaohongshu (vertical version) | Long-term search traffic, with vertical versions for wider reach |

Do not select them all by default. Explain each platform's output and the extra time it takes. When the user is short of time, keep only the main platform.

## What each platform is for

- X: testing ideas, industry discussion, curating resources and quick feedback.
- Xiaohongshu (RedNote): discovery through search, proof of process, image comparisons and an everyday tone.
- WeChat Official Account: full methods, cases, retrospectives and in-depth judgments.
- WeChat Channels: personality, reach inside the WeChat ecosystem and a real voice.
- Douyin: fast-paced demonstrations, broad reach and short-video search.
- YouTube: long-term search and recommendation, full landscape versions, and the search value of chapters and subtitles.
- Other platforms such as Bilibili: public research sources by default; add writing guidance only after the user chooses them.

## Not covered in this English edition

- Instagram and Facebook: there is no guidance for these image and short-text platforms yet. If you need them, follow the Xiaohongshu method in [xiaohongshu.md](../../self-media-platform-copywriting/references/xiaohongshu.md).
- TikTok: there is no separate guide; use `self-media-short-video`.
- LinkedIn: not covered.

## Exact specs

Character limits, image ratios, cover sizes, tag counts and publishing fields can change. When an exact value is needed, check first:

1. The platform's current official guidance for creators.
2. The fields and validation messages in the current publishing screen.
3. The project's existing brand or platform guidelines.

Do not treat past experience or numbers from attachments as permanent platform rules.
